# How SURREX works

SURREX converts community conviction into shipped products through one visible loop.

## 1. Propose

Members identify a product opportunity and submit a structured brief: the problem, intended users, proposed solution, strategic fit, risks, resources, and success criteria.

## 2. Deliberate

The community challenges assumptions before capital is committed. Feedback can narrow scope, surface dependencies, identify competing approaches, or show that an idea is not ready.

## 3. Vote

When a proposal meets the required standard, eligible token holders vote. A passing decision establishes a mandate; it does not erase execution, legal, security, or treasury constraints.

## 4. Execute

Surrex Labs translates the mandate into a delivery plan. Research, product, design, engineering, quality, security, and growth functions move the work from brief to launch.

## 5. Report and learn

Progress returns to the DAO through public updates. Material scope or budget changes should return to the appropriate governance path.

## 6. Compound

Each launch can add reusable technology, distribution, revenue, data, and operating knowledge. The goal is a flywheel in which one product improves the DAO's ability to select and ship the next.

| Layer | Primary role | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Source opportunities and improve briefs | Qualified proposals |
| Token holders | Set priorities and authorize mandates | Governance decisions |
| Surrex Labs | Build, launch, and operate | Shipped products |
| Treasury | Fund approved work under controls | Accountable allocation |
| Public reporting | Make progress and learning visible | Trust and feedback |
