539|# How SURREX works
540|
541|SURREX converts community conviction into shipped products through one visible loop.
542|
543|## 1. Propose
544|
545|Members identify a product opportunity and submit a structured brief: the problem, intended users, proposed solution, strategic fit, risks, resources, and success criteria.
546|
547|## 2. Deliberate
548|
549|The community challenges assumptions before capital is committed. Feedback can narrow scope, surface dependencies, identify competing approaches, or show that an idea is not ready.
550|
551|## 3. Vote
552|
553|When a proposal meets the required standard, eligible token holders vote. A passing decision establishes a mandate; it does not erase execution, legal, security, or treasury constraints.
554|
555|## 4. Execute
556|
557|Surrex Labs translates the mandate into a delivery plan. Research, product, design, engineering, quality, security, and growth functions move the work from brief to launch.
558|
559|## 5. Report and learn
560|
561|Progress returns to the DAO through public updates. Material scope or budget changes should return to the appropriate governance path.
562|
563|## 6. Compound
564|
565|Each launch can add reusable technology, distribution, revenue, data, and operating knowledge. The goal is a flywheel in which one product improves the DAO's ability to select and ship the next.
566|
567|| Layer | Primary role | Output |
568|| --- | --- | --- |
569|| Community | Source opportunities and improve briefs | Qualified proposals |
570|| Token holders | Set priorities and authorize mandates | Governance decisions |
571|| Surrex Labs | Build, launch, and operate | Shipped products |
572|| Treasury | Fund approved work under controls | Accountable allocation |
573|| Public reporting | Make progress and learning visible | Trust and feedback |
