## Verification policy

Before connecting a wallet or signing:

1. Confirm the domain character by character.
2. Navigate from an official property rather than a promoted search result.
3. Compare any contract address across multiple official SURREX sources.
4. Read the complete wallet request, including network, spender, amount, and permissions.
5. Reject unexpected signatures, approvals, token claims, or urgent support requests.

{% hint style="danger" %}
SURREX representatives should never ask for your seed phrase, private key, remote access, or an advance transfer to unlock an allocation.
{% endhint %}
