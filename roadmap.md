891|# Roadmap
892|
893|The roadmap follows one loop: prove the studio ships, hand the community the wheel, connect products to ecosystem value, and open the system to more builders.
894|
895|## Phase 1 - Proof layer
896|
897|**Status: now shipping**
898|
899|Establish public proof through live flagship products. Surrex Vibe Studio operates as both a product and the studio's execution engine.
900|
901|## Phase 2 - Governance layer
902|
903|**Status: next**
904|
905|Standardize proposals, deliberation, token-holder voting, and a public build pipeline in which community decisions become accountable mandates.
906|
907|## Phase 3 - Revenue layer
908|
909|**Status: planned**
910|
911|Define and implement approved mechanisms that connect product revenue, contributor rewards, idea-originator participation, treasury sustainability, and future builds.
912|
913|## Phase 4 - Builder ecosystem
914|
915|**Status: planned**
916|
917|Expand from one studio to a wider builder network. Community members and external teams can launch on SURREX rails while shared systems support product quality, launch, and distribution.
918|
919|## How progress should be judged
920|
921|Track whether the operating loop closes:
922|
923|- proposals become clear decisions;
924|- decisions become shipped software;
925|- shipped software attracts and retains users;
926|- products create sustainable value;
927|- value improves the ecosystem's capacity to build again.
928|
929|{% hint style="info" %}
930|Phases describe direction, not guaranteed dates. Governance, technical discoveries, market conditions, security findings, and legal requirements can change sequencing.
931|{% endhint %}
