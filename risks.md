# Risks & disclosures

Participation in crypto networks, governance, early-stage products, and digital assets involves substantial risk.

## Material risks

- **Product risk:** an approved product may be delayed, fail to find users, or never become sustainable.
- **Delivery risk:** scope, budget, staffing, dependencies, or technical complexity may prevent delivery.
- **Governance risk:** low participation, concentrated voting power, delegation capture, conflicts, or rushed decisions can produce poor outcomes.
- **Smart-contract risk:** code defects, oracle failures, compromised keys, bridge failures, or malicious integrations can cause permanent loss.
- **Treasury risk:** volatility, illiquidity, counterparty failure, operational mistakes, or inadequate controls can reduce runway.
- **Regulatory risk:** laws and enforcement positions vary by jurisdiction and can change.
- **Market risk:** token prices and liquidity can move sharply or disappear.
- **Security risk:** phishing, impersonation, malicious approvals, and wallet compromise are common.
- **Dependency risk:** products may rely on third-party models, chains, hosting, marketplaces, APIs, or policies.

## No guarantees

This documentation is descriptive and forward-looking. It does not create a guarantee that a token will launch, a proposal will pass, a product will ship, revenue will be generated or distributed, or any asset will gain value.

Nothing here is financial, investment, legal, accounting, or tax advice. Rules and availability may differ by jurisdiction. Obtain independent professional advice and never participate with funds you cannot afford to lose.

## Security basics

- Use only official links.
- Verify domains and contract addresses before signing.
- Read wallet transaction details and token approvals.
- Never share seed phrases or private keys.
- Use hardware wallets and separate governance wallets where appropriate.
- Treat unsolicited support messages as hostile.
