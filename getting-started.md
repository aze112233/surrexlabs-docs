579|# Join the DAO
580|
581|You do not need to begin with a proposal. The best first step is to understand the current governance state and contribute where the DAO already needs help.
582|
583|## Get started
584|
585|1. Visit [dao.surrexlabs.com](https://dao.surrexlabs.com).
586|2. Read active proposals and recent decisions.
587|3. Join the official [Telegram](https://t.me/surrexlabs) and follow [SURREX LABS on X](https://x.com/surrexlabs).
588|4. Contribute research, feedback, testing, design, engineering, distribution, or a product idea.
589|5. Verify every contract address and governance action against official sources before connecting a wallet or signing a transaction.
590|
591|## Ways to contribute
592|
593|- **Discover:** surface problems and market opportunities.
594|- **Evaluate:** pressure-test assumptions, budgets, risks, and success metrics.
595|- **Build:** contribute specialist work to approved initiatives when contributor paths are open.
596|- **Test:** use products, report defects, and improve onboarding.
597|- **Distribute:** help launches reach the communities they serve.
598|- **Govern:** vote or delegate when eligible.
599|
600|{% hint style="warning" %}
601|Never trust token addresses, airdrop claims, or wallet instructions from replies, private messages, unofficial groups, or search ads. SURREX will publish authoritative information through its official properties.
602|{% endhint %}
