744|# The execution layer
745|
746|The execution layer is SURREX's defining feature: a permanent product capability accountable to DAO-approved mandates.
747|
748|## From mandate to product
749|
750|After approval, the studio converts governance language into a build plan:
751|
752|1. **Frame** - validate the user, problem, constraints, and definition of success.
753|2. **Prototype** - make the riskiest assumptions testable quickly.
754|3. **Build** - deliver the smallest useful, secure product that meets the mandate.
755|4. **Verify** - test function, experience, security, and launch readiness.
756|5. **Launch** - publish with positioning, onboarding, analytics, and support.
757|6. **Operate** - measure results, maintain the product, and propose the next decision.
758|
759|## AI-native leverage
760|
761|Surrex Vibe Studio is both an ecosystem product and part of the studio's execution engine. It coordinates specialist AI functions across research, product management, design, engineering, quality, SEO, security, and growth.
762|
763|AI increases speed; it does not remove accountability. Material decisions, security-sensitive work, and release readiness still require appropriate human review.
764|
765|## Public accountability
766|
767|An approved initiative should expose:
768|
769|- owner and contributors;
770|- scope and current phase;
771|- milestone status;
772|- budget usage at an appropriate level;
773|- risks, blockers, and material changes;
774|- launch metrics and close-out learning.
775|
776|## Change control
777|
778|Small implementation choices belong to the accountable delivery team. Changes that materially alter cost, purpose, risk, or promised outcomes should return to governance or the authorized review path.
