# Governance framework

Governance is the operating system that connects member voice, prioritization, treasury decisions, product delivery, and accountability.

## Venture backing and formal governance

The updated pitch deck introduces $SRX backing as the intended product-selection mechanism. Holders stake behind ventures, and stronger conviction moves opportunities higher in the execution queue. Flexible backing allows support to move as conviction changes, subject to final implementation rules.

Formal governance provides the framework for budgets, mandates, policy changes, and accountability. The deck does not specify whether backing itself authorizes spending, whether a separate vote is required to greenlight a venture, or how conviction is calculated. These relationships must be explicit before the system is treated as binding.

The roles and safeguards below describe the governance framework to be finalized around that build-market model.

## Governance domains

The DAO may govern matters such as:

- product priorities and approved mandates;
- treasury allocation and operating budgets;
- governance parameters and working groups;
- ecosystem incentives and contributor programs;
- material changes to shared protocols or products;
- the progression toward greater decentralization.

## Roles

### Members

Members research opportunities, discuss proposals, provide domain expertise, test products, and contribute to the ecosystem.

### Proposers

Proposers own the quality of a brief. They respond to feedback, disclose conflicts, define outcomes, and keep the proposal current.

### Token holders and delegates

Eligible voters signal the DAO's preference under the rules active at the time of a vote. Delegation can help holders assign voting power to participants with relevant expertise and time.

### Venture backers

Backers direct $SRX support toward specific ventures under the proposed model. Their support influences product priority; any venture-economic participation depends on the final eligibility and revenue rules. Backing rights, voting rights, and delegation should not be assumed to be interchangeable.

### Surrex Labs

The studio turns approved mandates into delivery plans and working products. It is accountable for reporting progress and escalating material changes.

### Guardians or signers

Where used, security roles protect treasury or protocol operations through controls such as multisignature approval, timelocks, and emergency procedures. Their authority should be narrowly defined and publicly documented.

## Governance safeguards

- published proposal and voting requirements;
- conflict-of-interest disclosure;
- quorum and approval thresholds appropriate to proposal impact;
- treasury separation and multisignature controls;
- time for community review before implementation;
- public decision and implementation records;
- emergency powers that are limited, reviewable, and revocable.

{% hint style="info" %}
Exact voting thresholds, eligibility rules, delegation mechanics, and implementation delays must be taken from the live governance interface and approved governance documents. This preview does not replace them.
The same applies to backing amounts, custody, withdrawals, reallocation timing, greenlight criteria, and the authority to change the proposed revenue split.
{% endhint %}
