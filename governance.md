608|# Governance framework
609|
610|Governance is the operating system that connects member voice, prioritization, treasury intent, execution, and accountability.
611|
612|## Governance domains
613|
614|The DAO may govern matters such as:
615|
616|- product priorities and approved mandates;
617|- treasury allocation and operating budgets;
618|- governance parameters and working groups;
619|- ecosystem incentives and contributor programs;
620|- material changes to shared protocols or products;
621|- the progression toward greater decentralization.
622|
623|## Roles
624|
625|### Members
626|
627|Members research opportunities, discuss proposals, provide domain expertise, test products, and contribute to the ecosystem.
628|
629|### Proposers
630|
631|Proposers own the quality of a brief. They respond to feedback, disclose conflicts, define outcomes, and keep the proposal current.
632|
633|### Token holders and delegates
634|
635|Eligible voters signal the DAO's preference under the rules active at the time of a vote. Delegation can help holders assign voting power to participants with relevant expertise and time.
636|
637|### Surrex Labs
638|
639|The studio turns approved mandates into delivery plans and product execution. It is accountable for reporting progress and escalating material changes.
640|
641|### Guardians or signers
642|
643|Where used, security roles protect treasury or protocol operations through controls such as multisignature approval, timelocks, and emergency procedures. Their authority should be narrowly defined and publicly documented.
644|
645|## Governance safeguards
646|
647|- published proposal and voting requirements;
648|- conflict-of-interest disclosure;
649|- quorum and approval thresholds appropriate to proposal impact;
650|- treasury separation and multisignature controls;
651|- time for community review before execution;
652|- public decision and implementation records;
653|- emergency powers that are limited, reviewable, and revocable.
654|
655|{% hint style="info" %}
656|Exact voting thresholds, eligibility rules, delegation mechanics, and execution delays must be taken from the live governance interface and approved governance documents. This preview does not replace them.
657|{% endhint %}
