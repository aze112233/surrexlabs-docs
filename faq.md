974|## What is SURREX LABS DAO?
975|
976|SURREX is a product DAO with an execution layer. The community proposes opportunities, eligible token holders make governance decisions, and Surrex Labs turns approved mandates into products.
977|
978|## How is it different from a grant DAO?
979|
980|The core model includes a persistent studio accountable for product delivery. Funding can still involve external contributors, but governance is connected to an operating system for research, design, engineering, launch, and iteration.
981|
982|## Can anyone submit an idea?
983|
984|The intended model is open participation, subject to the eligibility, moderation, and anti-spam rules active on the official governance platform.
985|
986|## Does a passing vote guarantee a launch?
987|
988|No. A passing vote creates a mandate under the approved scope and controls. Technical feasibility, security, law, third-party dependencies, and material new information can require changes or a return to governance.
989|
