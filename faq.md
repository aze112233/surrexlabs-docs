# FAQ

## What is SURREX LABS DAO?

SURREX is a community-governed build market connected to a product studio. Members propose opportunities, $SRX holders back ventures they believe deserve execution, and Surrex researches, builds, launches, and iterates on selected products.

## How is it different from a grant DAO?

The model includes a persistent delivery team and AI execution system. Community direction connects to research, planning, development, testing, deployment, and ongoing product improvement. See [The DAO thesis](dao-thesis.md).

## What does backing a venture mean?

The deck proposes staking $SRX behind a specific venture to express conviction and influence its priority in the execution queue. It does not specify minimum amounts, custody, lock periods, or withdrawal rules. See [How SURREX works](how-it-works.md).

## Can I move my backing?

Flexible backing is part of the proposed utility: support can move between ventures as conviction changes. Timing, restrictions, and the effect on economic eligibility remain to be defined. The deck does not promise instant withdrawal.

## Is backing the same as a governance vote?

Backing is the proposed venture-priority mechanism. Formal governance covers decisions such as budgets and rule changes under the applicable framework. The deck does not define how backing and formal votes interact or whether delegation applies to backing.

## Does enough backing or a passing vote guarantee launch?

No. Higher conviction moves a venture higher in the intended queue, but the deck gives no automatic selection threshold. Delivery remains subject to capacity, feasibility, the approved mandate, and required reviews.

## How would venture revenue be shared?

The proposed split is **50% venture backers, 25% $SRX buybacks, 24% Surrex treasury, and 1% idea originator**. The deck says these percentages are tunable as the DAO and its legal and economic structure mature. The revenue basis and payout rules are not defined. See [Treasury & value flow](treasury-and-value.md).

## Do all $SRX holders receive the backer allocation?

No automatic entitlement is established. The proposal designates the 50% allocation for venture backers, and participation depends on the final structure. It does not give every token holder a share or define each backer's calculation.

## What does an idea originator receive?

The proposed model allocates 1% of a successful venture's revenue to the community member who originated it. Attribution, eligibility, duration, and payment terms must be defined before this becomes an operative program.

## Are buybacks the same as burning tokens?

The deck proposes routing 25% of venture revenue toward $SRX buybacks. It does not specify whether purchased tokens are burned, held, or reused.

## What are the token allocation and vesting plans?

The deck shows 35% company treasury, 19% PROOF Bundle Snipe, and 46% liquidity pool. Alongside it, the slide lists 25% locked for 6 months then vested linearly over 12 months, 8% in a group multisignature wallet, and 2% team float at launch. The relationship between these portions and the treasury allocation needs explicit confirmation. See [The $SRX token](token.md).

## Can anyone submit an idea?

The intended model welcomes community ideas, subject to the eligibility, moderation, and anti-spam rules active on the official platform.

## What is the $SRX contract address, and where can I buy it?

Neither the supplied deck nor this repository provides a verified contract address, launch date, or trading venue. Refer to official SURREX properties for authoritative launch information.

## What is Surrex Builder?

It is the self-serve building path described in the deck. The existing documentation introduces the AI builder as Surrex Vibe Studio at [app.surrexlabs.com](https://app.surrexlabs.com). Self-serve use does not by itself place a product in the DAO-backed venture program.

## Who builds the selected ventures?

The deck identifies Azee as Founder / Product Lead, Josh as Community Lead / Assistant Developer, and Surrex Execution AI as the supporting execution system, with an expandable specialist network. See [Team & execution system](team.md).

## Where do I find active rules and progress?

Use [dao.surrexlabs.com](https://dao.surrexlabs.com) and approved governance records for operative rules. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) describes the deck's intended sequence, including public progress tracking and ecosystem reporting.
