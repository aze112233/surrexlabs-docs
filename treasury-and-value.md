784|# Treasury & value flow
785|
786|The treasury turns governance decisions into resourced work. Its design should protect community assets while allowing approved teams to execute.
787|
788|## Intended value loop
789|
790|**Community insight -> approved mandate -> funded execution -> shipped product -> users and revenue -> ecosystem capacity -> new mandates**
791|
792|Products may create value through subscriptions, usage fees, transaction-linked fees, licensing, services, partnerships, or other models appropriate to the product. The exact routing of any revenue depends on the product, entity structure, agreements, applicable law, and approved governance policy.
793|
794|## Treasury principles
795|
796|- **Purpose-bound allocation:** every transfer maps to an approved use.
797|- **Least privilege:** signers and operators receive only the access required.
798|- **Staged funding:** large initiatives can unlock resources by milestone.
799|- **Transparency:** addresses, policies, approvals, and reports should be public where safe and lawful.
800|- **Diversification:** treasury risk should not depend on one volatile asset or counterparty.
801|- **Runway discipline:** the DAO should understand ongoing obligations before funding new work.
802|
803|## Contributor and originator incentives
804|
805|SURREX intends to align idea originators, contributors, operators, and the broader ecosystem around useful outcomes. Any reward or revenue-share program must define eligibility, calculation, vesting or timing, termination, tax treatment, and legal constraints before participants rely on it.
806|
807|{% hint style="warning" %}
808|No statement in this preview guarantees revenue, distributions, token appreciation, liquidity, or a financial return. Product outcomes and digital assets are high risk.
809|{% endhint %}
