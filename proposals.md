663|# Proposal lifecycle
664|
665|A strong proposal makes a decision easier to evaluate and an approved mandate easier to execute.
666|
667|## Stage 1: Signal
668|
669|Share the problem and evidence early. The goal is to find whether the community considers the opportunity relevant before substantial work is spent on a formal proposal.
670|
671|## Stage 2: Draft
672|
673|Use a consistent structure:
674|
675|1. **Summary** - the decision requested in plain language.
676|2. **Problem** - who experiences it and what evidence supports it.
677|3. **Proposed product** - the smallest credible solution.
678|4. **Strategic fit** - why SURREX should act.
679|5. **Scope and milestones** - what will and will not be delivered.
680|6. **Resources** - budget, people, infrastructure, and dependencies.
681|7. **Success metrics** - measurable adoption, delivery, or economic outcomes.
682|8. **Risks** - technical, market, legal, security, and governance concerns.
683|9. **Reporting** - update cadence and accountable owner.
684|10. **Conflicts** - proposer relationships or potential benefits.
685|
686|## Stage 3: Review
687|
688|The community tests the draft. High-impact proposals may require specialist, security, legal, or treasury review before becoming eligible for a vote.
689|
690|## Stage 4: Vote
691|
692|The final text should freeze before voting begins. Voters must be able to see exactly what is authorized, including the requested resources and success criteria.
693|
694|## Stage 5: Execute
695|
696|After approval, the mandate enters the public build pipeline. A proposal may define staged funding so later resources depend on accepted milestones.
697|
698|## Stage 6: Close or renew
699|
700|Completed work receives a close-out report. A new vote may be required for material expansion, additional budget, or long-term operation.
701|
702|{% hint style="warning" %}
703|A passing vote is not a promise of investment return or a waiver of applicable law, security review, technical feasibility, or third-party platform rules.
704|{% endhint %}
