# SURREX LABS DAO

**Community-governed products, built to ship.**

SURREX LABS is a product DAO with an execution layer. Members surface opportunities, token holders decide what earns support, and the Surrex studio turns approved mandates into working products.

The objective is simple: make governance produce outcomes. A proposal should not end as a forum thread. It should move through a visible pipeline from idea to decision, build, launch, and measurable learning.

{% hint style="info" %}
SURREX is being developed in phases. Governance rules, token parameters, treasury controls, and product-revenue mechanics become authoritative only when published through official SURREX channels and approved through the applicable governance process.
{% endhint %}

## The operating loop

1. **Propose** - a member describes a real problem, audience, product concept, and expected value.
2. **Deliberate** - the community tests assumptions and improves the brief.
3. **Vote** - eligible token holders authorize priority and resources.
4. **Execute** - Surrex Labs scopes, prototypes, builds, and ships in public.
5. **Share** - products generate learning, infrastructure, and potential revenue for the ecosystem.

## Why SURREX exists

Many DAOs can coordinate attention and capital but struggle to turn decisions into polished software. Studios can ship software but usually concentrate direction and upside in a small team. SURREX connects community governance to a permanent, AI-native product studio.

> Governance without execution is a forum. Execution without governance is just another studio.
