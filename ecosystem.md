856|# Products & ecosystem
857|
858|SURREX pairs a community-governed core with an AI-native studio and live product surfaces.
859|
860|## Surrex DAO
861|
862|The governance layer at [dao.surrexlabs.com](https://dao.surrexlabs.com) is where community direction becomes structured proposals and decisions.
863|
864|## Surrex Vibe Studio
865|
866|[Surrex Vibe Studio](https://app.surrexlabs.com) is an AI product builder and evidence of the studio's execution capability. A user describes a product in plain language; specialist agents help research, scope, design, build, verify, and ship it.
867|
868|Vibe Studio supports multiple product tracks, including websites, mobile apps, full-stack applications, browser extensions, and playable browser games. Within the DAO model, the same execution system can give approved ideas more speed and leverage.
869|
870|## Surrex Studios
871|
872|[Surrex Studios](https://surrex.space) develops interactive worlds, including SURREALVERSE. It extends the ecosystem beyond conventional apps and demonstrates product craft across games and digital experiences.

874|## The compounding model
875|
876|Each product can contribute one or more assets back to the ecosystem:
877|
878|- recurring or transaction-linked revenue;
879|- reusable software and infrastructure;
880|- audience and distribution;
881|- proprietary workflows and operating knowledge;
882|- contributor opportunities;
883|- evidence that improves future governance decisions.
884|
885|The DAO decides where collective resources should go. The studio supplies execution. Products provide the test.
